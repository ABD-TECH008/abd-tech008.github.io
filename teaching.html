Teaching

Maths Tutor · 3 years · Classes 6 to 10

What I did

For three years I taught Mathematics to students of Classes 6 to 10, in tuition classes both offline and online. I explained new chapters, solved doubts and helped students prepare for their school exams.

How I taught

I broke each topic into small steps, worked through examples on the board, and then gave students practice questions to try themselves. Online classes taught me to explain ideas clearly without relying on gestures, and to keep students engaged on screen.

What I learned

Teaching taught me patience and communication. Explaining a concept in three different ways until it clicks is the same skill I use now to explain my code to teammates.
